# Reviewer recruitment and assignment guidance

The completed expanded study used eight initial raters in four fixed dyads,
a separate source follow-up, and a separate adjudication assignment. The
earlier two-account allocation is historical. No additional ratings are
requested for the frozen dataset through this document.

For a future replication, document the actual allocation and rubric before
collecting responses, obtain the applicable oversight and participation
permissions, provide the issued neutral task guide, and keep identities,
consent records and assignment credentials private. Record substantive
allocation changes as dated amendments.

## Ready-to-send invitation

Please compare the provided source and rewritten passages and record meaning
preservation, factual changes, event order, speaker relationships, tone and
usability using the supplied rating definitions. Complete your assigned packet
independently and return your responses. Participation, privacy and data-sharing
information will be provided before the work begins.

## If university support is needed

Contact the appropriate departmental research coordinator for recruitment and
ethics routing. This guide does not establish approval, consent or participant
independence. The completed study's unresolved permissions are described in
`audit/ACCESS_AND_EXCLUSIONS.md`.
