# Meaning-preservation review: reviewer guide

This guide accompanies your private assignment in the local Review Desk. Your
packet contains 1,620 pairs of original and rewritten fiction passages. The
order and item IDs are specific to your assignment. Do not share your link,
ratings, notes, or completed return with another reviewer.

Read both passages in full. Judge how well the rewrite preserves the original's
meaning, not which version you prefer. Use the definitions shown in the Review
Desk:

- Added facts, omitted facts, changed event order, and changed relationships:
  choose **No (0)** or **Yes (1)**. Relationships include speakers, characters,
  ownership, causes, and who did what.
- Tone drift: **0** = none; **1** = noticeable but not fundamental; **2** = a
  substantial change in emotion, seriousness, irony, or narrative stance.
- Meaning preservation: **5** = all material meaning retained; **4** = minor
  nonmaterial deviations; **3** = localized material distortion; **2** = several
  material distortions; **1** = meaning largely changed or missing.
- Usable for style comparison: choose **Yes** only when meaning changes do not
  materially confound a comparison of stylistic change.
- Notes: identify the affected claim, event, speaker, or relationship. Notes are
  required for a factual, order, or relationship change; a meaning score of 1–3;
  or an unusable rewrite.

Form every rating yourself. Do not consult anyone else's ratings or use a tool
or service to decide or generate answers for you. Accessibility aids are fine
when they do not make the judgments. You may pause and return later; use **Save**
or **Save & next** so your work is retained. Do not alter the displayed passages
or item IDs. If uncertain, record the uncertainty in your notes rather than
guessing to finish quickly.

When all items are complete, use **Check missing responses**, then **Finish
review** and **Submit my review**. Submit only your own completed work. The
original submitted return and submission time are preserved; later corrections
must be documented separately.

The interface omits source identities and processing details to keep ratings
independent. The investigator provides any required study information, consent,
payment, and contact arrangements separately; this guide is not a consent form.
