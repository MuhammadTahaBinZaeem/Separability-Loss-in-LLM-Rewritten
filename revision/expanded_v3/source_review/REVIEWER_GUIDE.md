# Source-integrity follow-up: reviewer guide

This guide accompanies your four-passage follow-up assignment in the local
Review Desk. The earlier source review is preserved separately. Use your own
private link and do not share your answers or access card.

For each passage, read the entire selected text and inspect its archived source
context, including both boundaries. Check that the span belongs to the
specified work's fictional body and is correctly attributed. Look for
introductions, editorial narration, footnotes, heading fragments, borrowed
compositions, and mixed or uncertain authorship. Distinguish dialogue within
the fiction from material written outside the fiction. You can download the
full archived source from the Review Desk when more context is needed.

Choose **Pass** only when the passage is suitable and correctly attributed.
Choose **Needs correction / uncertain** when you find a problem or cannot
resolve a doubt. Write a substantive note for every item explaining what you
checked, and identify any exact problematic words or boundaries. Do not mark an
uncertain passage clean simply to complete the packet.

Form every judgment yourself. Do not use a tool or service to decide or
generate your verdicts. Accessibility aids are fine when they do not make the
judgments. Use **Save** or **Save & next** as you work. Do not alter the passage,
source identifiers, or offsets. When all four items are complete, use **Check
missing responses**, then **Finish review** and **Submit my review**. Submit
only your own completed work. The original submitted return and submission time
are preserved; a later correction must be documented separately.

The investigator provides any required study information, consent, payment,
and contact arrangements separately; this guide is not a consent form.
