# Focused literature and novelty check

Checked 2026-09-21 against publisher/author records. This is a focused update,
not a systematic review or proof that no closer paper exists.

| Primary source | Relevance | Distinction that the manuscript must retain |
|---|---|---|
| [Altakrori et al., 2021](https://aclanthology.org/2021.findings-emnlp.359/) | Topic-confusion evaluation of attribution | Work holdout does not eliminate all topic-author confounding |
| [Altakrori et al., 2022](https://aclanthology.org/2022.emnlp-main.153/) | Separate evasion, preservation and misattribution evaluation | Attribution loss is not preservation evidence |
| [Wang et al., 2023](https://aclanthology.org/2023.tacl-1.80/) | Interrogates what authorship representations capture | Our lexical feature set is not a pure or exhaustive style measure |
| [Tripto et al., 2024](https://aclanthology.org/2024.acl-long.357/) | Attribution under repeated paraphrasing | No claim that paraphrasing-related attribution loss is newly discovered |
| [O'Sullivan, 2025](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41599-025-05986-3) | Creative stories; frequent-word distances and clustering | New composition differs from same-source paired rewriting |
| [Kirilloff et al., 2025](https://doi.org/10.1162/99608f92.6d5fb5ef) | Named-style generation and nineteenth-century fiction | Explicit imitation differs from instructions withholding author labels |
| [Icard et al., 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.10606v1) | French literary style transfer and embedding dispersion | Closest recent precedent; includes topic control and different representations |

Consequences: the defensible contribution is an independently reproducible,
source-traceable English literary transfer experiment with work holdout,
train-fold-only preprocessing, explicit service refusal accounting and paired
preservation auditing. A new model/date or larger row count is not by itself a
major conceptual contribution. Top-journal suitability remains an editorial and
scientific judgment, not a software-validation flag.
